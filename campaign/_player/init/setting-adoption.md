# Setting Adoption

## Explicit Campaign Decision

- **Canonical setting:** The Quaternary Sector from *The Shattered Reach*
- **Present date:** 3500 CE
- **Primary mechanics:** *Loner: Spacer*, with Loner Core Oracle rules
- **Supplemental material:** *Galaxy Drifter* port life, shipboard culture, debts, favors, work, rumors, cargo, and encounter inspiration

## Existing-Fiction Placement

- Wraith Base is in the Contested Zone near the Outer Reaches.
- The provisional Planetary Coalition was collapsed into the Concordat; Commander de Le Goff represents a Concordat liaison office at Wraith Base.
- The Cinder Ledger operates in the independent underworld.
- The origin of the Charybdis Rift threat remains unresolved.

## Retcon

The provisional archive year `3207` was replaced with `3500 CE` to align campaign records with the present day of *The Shattered Reach*. Event month, day, time, causal order, and established fiction did not change. The setting defines no finer universal calendar, so those components remain campaign archive conventions.
