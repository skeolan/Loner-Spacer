## Established Interpretation

- **Location:** Wraith Base, a station beside the Charybdis Rift.
- **Anomaly:** The Charybdis Rift is an unstable knot of distorted starlight.
- **Threat:** Three survey vessels have been lost near the Rift. Something emerging from it destroyed the latest, the *Argent Surveyor*, and killed thirty-one people.
- **Contracting faction:** The Concordat.
- **Patron:** Commander Dorothée de Le Goff.
- **Objective:** Recover the *Argent Surveyor*'s ejected navigation core, whose attack telemetry may reveal how to eliminate the unknown threat.
- **Reward:** Hyperfuel, departure clearance, and a military-grade energy core installed aboard the *Horizon Chaser*.
- **Legal complication:** Marshal Dafne Catalina Castrillo Guitart has suspended Nova's departure clearance and ordered an inspection.
- **Evidence at risk:** The empty smuggler's hold contains the Cinder Ledger's encrypted payment records.
- **Immediate pressure:** Officers are waiting outside the sealed airlock while Nova presses Commander de Le Goff to clear the Port Authority blockade.

## Supporting Extrapolations

- Nova is nearly broke and needs hyperfuel before departure.
- Nova requested departure clearance first thing that morning; it was still pending when Commander de Le Goff called.
- Nova was finishing the *Horizon Chaser*'s final preflight and planned to scrounge hyperfuel on Wraith Base next.
- The Cinder Ledger expects its next payment within seventy-two hours.
- The *Horizon Chaser*'s advanced navigation suite can locate the ejected core through the Rift's distortion.
- The encrypted records contain enough names to provoke a major conflict if exposed.
- The *Horizon Chaser* is physically restrained by Wraith Base's docking clamps.
- Whether Commander de Le Goff knew of or influenced the Port Authority inspection is not established.
