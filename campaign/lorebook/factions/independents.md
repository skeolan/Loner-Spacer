# Independents

- **Concept:** Traders, smugglers, mercenaries, pirates, and unaffiliated operators
- **Strengths:** Mobility, flexibility, access across faction lines
- **Frailty:** Little collective protection or central authority
- **Current posture:** Operate throughout the Contested Zone between major powers

## Campaign Connections

- Nova Darkreach operates as an independent courier.
- The [Cinder Ledger](cinder-ledger.md) operates within the independent underworld.
