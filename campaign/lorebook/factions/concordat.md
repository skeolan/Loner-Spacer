# Concordat

- **Concept:** Alliance of wealthy Core Worlds
- **Strengths:** Advanced technology, economic power, FTL access, trade control
- **Frailty:** Exploitation and deepening inequality
- **Current posture:** Preserves order while competing with the Frontier League

## Campaign Connections

- Commander [Dorothée de Le Goff](../npcs/commander-dorothee-de-le-goff.md) represents a Concordat liaison office operating at Wraith Base.
- Concordat Survey Command lost the *Argent Surveyor* near the Charybdis Rift and designated its navigation core for recovery.
- The Wraith Base liaison office transmitted the contract offer to Nova on the Concordat's behalf.
