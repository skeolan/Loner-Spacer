# Charybdis Rift

- **Type:** Deep-space anomaly
- **Region:** Contested Zone near the Outer Reaches
- **Description:** An unstable knot of distorted starlight
- **Current hazard:** An unknown threat is emerging from it

## Established Facts

- Three survey vessels have been lost nearby.
- The latest, the *Argent Surveyor*, was destroyed by the threat associated with the Rift.
- No connection to the Predecessors or Elder Dominion is established.
