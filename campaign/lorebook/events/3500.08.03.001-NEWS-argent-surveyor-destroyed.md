# Survey Command Incident Bulletin

> **VESSEL LOSS CONFIRMED**
>
> Survey vessel *Argent Surveyor* was destroyed during operations near the Charybdis Rift. Thirty-one crew are recorded dead. No recovery signal has been authenticated.
>
> The destructive agent remains unidentified. Survey Command attributes the loss to a threat emerging from the Rift pending recovery of the vessel's navigation core.
>
> Recovery of the navigation core is designated a priority objective for all authorized operations in the exclusion zone.
>
> All civilian traffic is advised to maintain distance and await revised hazard boundaries.
