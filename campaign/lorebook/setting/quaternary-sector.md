# Quaternary Sector

- **Canonical setting:** *The Shattered Reach*
- **Present date:** 3500 CE
- **Campaign region:** Wraith Base lies in the Contested Zone near the Outer Reaches
- **Political climate:** Fragile peace between the Concordat and Frontier League; Independents operate between and beyond them

## Cultural Premise

Humanity descends from Earth refugees carried by the Longreach Arks. Ark populations merged, separated, migrated, and developed in isolation across asynchronous worlds. Modern locale datasets are therefore naming source pools, not intact future nationalities or reliable markers of ancestry.

## Unresolved

The origin and nature of the Charybdis Rift threat remain unknown. Play may reveal a connection to the Predecessors, Elder Dominion, or something else.
