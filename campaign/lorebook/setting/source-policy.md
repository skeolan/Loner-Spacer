# Campaign Source Policy

Use sources in this order when they conflict:

1. Established campaign files and explicit player decisions
2. *Loner: Spacer* for character, ship, economy, cargo, travel, and trade mechanics
3. *Loner Core* for the Oracle and general gameplay loop
4. *The Shattered Reach* for canonical history, geography, cultures, factions, and political milieu
5. *Galaxy Drifter* for optional port life, shipboard culture, debts, favors, work, rumors, cargo, and encounter inspiration

Do not import Galaxy Drifter's default search-for-Earth premise or named factions unless play establishes them. Use its tables and cultural material to enrich gaps without duplicating Shattered Reach organizations.
